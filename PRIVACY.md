# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Simple Podcast Manager is a community project. It is not built to gather information about you, track your behavior, or make money from your activity.

## What we collect

Nothing. Simple Podcast Manager has no accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or developer-operated crash reporting. The developer does not receive your podcast library, listening activity, downloads, settings, or device contents. There is no Simple Podcast Manager server collecting this information.

Your podcasts, settings, episode history, and download records are stored locally on your Mac. Exports and backups are created only when you request them and remain wherever you save them.

## When the app connects to the internet

Simple Podcast Manager makes only the connections needed to provide its features:

- When you search for a podcast, your search terms are sent directly to [Podcast Index](https://podcastindex.org). Podcast Index also receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address, the request time, and the app's name.
- When podcasts are refreshed or episodes and artwork are downloaded, the app connects directly to the podcast publishers and hosting providers. Those services can receive ordinary connection information and see which files are requested.
- When you check for updates, or enable automatic update checks, the app contacts Simple Podcast Manager's pages and releases hosted by GitHub. Anonymous system profiling is not enabled.

These third-party services handle information under their own privacy policies. Simple Podcast Manager does not add tracking to these requests or receive reports about them.

## Our commitment

We do not sell, rent, or use personal information for advertising or profiling. If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Questions or concerns are welcome through the [Simple Podcast Manager issue tracker](https://github.com/steveneely/simple-podcast-manager/issues).
