Setup

Five steps from fresh install to synced podcasts.

  1. Install the app. Download the DMG from GitHub Releases, open it, and drag the app to Applications.
  2. Add podcast feeds. Paste RSS feed URLs for the shows you want to keep on your player.
  3. Download episodes. Choose individual episodes or prepare all loaded episodes for a show.
  4. Plug in your MP3 player. The device needs to mount in Finder and have a top-level folder named music.
  5. Review and start. Check the sync plan, then let the app copy managed podcast files to the device.

A quiet app for a repeated chore.

The interface keeps the important state visible: subscribed shows, prepared episodes, detected device, and the exact work that will happen during sync.

Device Safety

The sync plan is the contract.

Simple Podcast Manager is intentionally cautious. It plans first, shows you the intended changes, validates paths, and only performs device actions that fit those rules.

  • Only writes under the device's music folder.
  • Only deletes app-managed podcast files.
  • Aborts destructive work when validation is uncertain.

Common Questions

Short answers for people deciding whether this fits their player and podcast habits.

  • Does it require Spotify or Apple Podcasts?

    No. Simple Podcast Manager works from podcast RSS feed URLs and keeps downloaded audio local.

  • What devices work?

    The device should mount in Finder as an external volume and have a top-level music folder. That can include many Sony Walkman players, SanDisk Clip players, AGPTEK players, and other simple USB MP3 players.

  • Do I need an account?

    No. There is no hosted service, login, or sync account.

  • Is it free and open source?

    Yes. Simple Podcast Manager is free and open source under the MIT License.

  • Why do I need to set an ffmpeg path?

    Most MP3 podcast episodes work without ffmpeg. Some podcasts publish audio in other formats, and Simple Podcast Manager uses ffmpeg to convert those files into MP3s. Current builds do not bundle ffmpeg, so you can point the app at an installed copy if you need conversion.

  • Is it signed and notarized?

    Current prerelease builds may require manual approval in macOS Privacy & Security. Release signing and notarization are planned.

  • Where should I start?

    Download the latest release, add one RSS feed, prepare one episode, and try a sync with a device that has a music folder.
