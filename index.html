Quick Start

From RSS feed to MP3 player.

  1. Install. Open the DMG and drag the app to Applications.
  2. Add a show. Search for a podcast, or paste its RSS feed URL.
  3. Download episodes. Download new episodes after feed refreshes, or choose individual episodes yourself.
  4. Connect your player. Plug it in.
  5. Sync. Review the plan, then click Sync.

Device Safety

Review every change before syncing.

The app shows the complete plan before it copies or removes anything.

  • Writes only inside the device’s podcast folder.
  • Deletes app-managed episodes only when selected.
  • Stops when device paths cannot be validated.

Common questions

  • What devices work?

    USB MP3 players that mount in Finder as external volumes.

  • Do I need an account?

    No. Search for podcasts directly or add a show with its RSS feed URL; Spotify and Apple Podcasts are not required.

  • Do I need ffmpeg?

    Only when a podcast publishes audio in a format other than MP3.

  • Is it signed and notarized?

    Not yet. If macOS blocks the first launch, allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
